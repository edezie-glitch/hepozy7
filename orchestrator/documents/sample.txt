Hepozy Support Hours

Hepozy's support team is available Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm Philippine Standard Time. Support requests submitted outside these hours are answered on the next business day.

Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if they have not exceeded their monthly message quota. Refund requests are processed within 3 to 5 business days.

Data Retention

Chat conversation history is retained for 90 days unless the user requests earlier deletion. Deleted conversations cannot be recovered.
