FAQ

Why do I have to verify my email?

After creating an account you are sent to a verification step. You can't log in until your email is confirmed.

What are the password requirements?

At least 20 characters (a short sentence works well). Both password fields must match.

Why can't I log in?

Log in with your username, not your email. If you just registered, finish email verification first.
